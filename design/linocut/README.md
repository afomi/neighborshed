# Linocut (study)

A second illustration style, tried on 2026-11-24 against ink and wash: a two-colour linocut block print in leaf green and rose pink on warm cream paper, with bold carved black lines.
Not adopted yet; nothing on the site uses it.

Why try it: bold shapes and solid colour stay readable at small sizes, on a phone, in a Facebook feed and on a printed flyer, where ink and wash's fine lines can fade.
It also feels handmade and neighborly, like a community bulletin board.

Same recipe as `../ink-and-wash/README.md`, with this style block in place of that one:

```text
Two-colour linocut block print on warm cream paper: bold hand-carved black lines with flat areas of leaf green and rose pink, visible carving marks and a little ink texture, simple bold shapes that read clearly at a small size. <SUBJECT>. Plain cream background, no shadows. No faces, no text, no letters, no numbers.
```

The cream paper is part of the look, so these are not keyed to transparent; place them as a card.
