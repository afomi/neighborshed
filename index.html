Share what you have with neighbors.

Your street already has a ladder, a mower and some folding tables. NeighborShed is a simple set of ground rules for lending them to each other. It covers how to ask, how to give things back, and what to do if something breaks.

Read the ground rules Use them in your group or app

Take good care of what you borrow. Keep in touch, bring it back on time, and if it breaks, work it out with the owner.

Lending works when everyone knows the rules

Most streets have plenty of tools. What's missing is trust that a loan will go well. That the drill comes back charged. That you'll hear if it's running late. That a broken blade leads to a fair talk, not a feud.

When everyone knows the rules, saying yes is easy.

Works with or without an app

You can follow these rules on paper, in a group chat, in a city program, or in an app. Each rule has a short number, like NS-7, so a group can point to the ones it follows.

They're free for anyone to use.

Bring them to your group

Do you run a tool library, a neighborhood group, a city loan program, or an app? You're welcome to use these rules and add your name to the list. Anyone can suggest a change.
