Share what you have with neighbors.

Your street already has a ladder, a mower and some folding tables. NeighborShed is a simple set of ground rules for lending them to each other. It covers how to ask, how to give things back, and what to do if something breaks.

Take good care of what you borrow. Keep in touch, bring it back on time, and if it breaks, work it out with the owner.

Put tools to good use

Qart

Have an item to share with neighbors?

Qart is an app for keeping a list of the things you own. Sharing with neighbors is one thing you can do with that list.

  1. Sign up at qart.app.
  2. Add an item. Your list stays private.
  3. Share it with people nearby, when you're ready.
Use qart.app

It implements NeighborShed sharing.
